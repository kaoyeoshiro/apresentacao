SERIES STYLE: quiet low-key still-life photography of everyday study and desk work, black-and-white print look with deep, soft shadows and open midtones in the lit areas. One soft directional light from the upper left, gentle falloff into darkness; most of the frame is a smooth dark matte backdrop. 50mm lens look, f/5.6, camera level or pointing straight down, no tilt. Materials: plain paper notebooks, blank index cards, loose A4 sheets, a graphite pencil, binder clips, a plain ceramic mug, a dark wooden desk. Fine, barely visible film grain, natural contrast, no HDR, no lens flare, no bokeh balls, no haze. No text, letters, numbers, logos, emblems or watermarks; paper shows at most faint grey lines. No people, faces or hands. No light bulbs, gears, brains, rockets, targets with arrows, puzzle pieces, trophies, hourglasses or neon.
